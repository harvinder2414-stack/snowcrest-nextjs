Luxe Vista sits ten kilometres above the Dalhousie highway, in Banikhet — close enough to reach without a plan, far enough to actually feel like the hills.

## Four Room Categories

Every room at Luxe Vista falls into one of four categories, sorted by what you'll actually wake up to:

- **King Mountain View** — a king bed facing a full mountain-facing window
- **Rooms Scenic View** — balcony access with a valley-facing window
- **Panorama Balcony** — our widest sightline, a full panoramic window paired with a balcony
- **Family Suite** — two double beds and a panoramic balcony, built for groups

## Direct Booking, No Middleman

You can check availability and send a booking request straight to the property — over WhatsApp, by phone, or by email — with no OTA commission built into the price.
