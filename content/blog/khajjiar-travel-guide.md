Khajjiar is a saucer-shaped meadow in Himachal Pradesh's Chamba district, with a small lake sitting at its centre and cedar forest ringing the edges. In 1992, a visiting Swiss diplomat compared its landscape to the Swiss countryside, and the "Mini Switzerland of India" nickname has stuck ever since.

## How Far Is Khajjiar From Banikhet or Dalhousie?

Khajjiar sits roughly 20–24 km from Dalhousie town by road, and about the same distance from Banikhet — a drive of 45 minutes to an hour depending on traffic and season. If you're staying at [Luxe Vista by Snow Crest](/luxe-vista) in Banikhet, Khajjiar makes an easy half-day or full-day trip without needing to change accommodation.

## Best Time to Visit

Late March through June and again from September to November give the clearest skies and easiest road conditions. Winter (December–February) brings snow that can be beautiful but also makes some approach roads difficult — check local conditions before planning a winter visit.

## Top Things to Do

1. **Khajjiar Lake & the Meadow** — the centrepiece of Khajjiar, best enjoyed with a slow walk.
2. **Adventure Activities** — horse riding, zorbing, and tandem paragliding operate directly off the meadow.
3. **Khajji Nag Temple** — a 12th-century wooden temple known for its golden dome.
4. **Kalatop Wildlife Sanctuary** — a cedar-and-fir forest reserve on the Khajjiar–Dalhousie road.
5. **Dainkund Peak Trek** — a moderate trek to the highest point near Dalhousie, with wide valley views.

## Where to Stay Near Khajjiar

Khajjiar itself has limited accommodation, so most travellers base themselves in Dalhousie or Banikhet and visit as a day trip. [Luxe Vista by Snow Crest](/contact#contact) sits in Banikhet, roughly 20 km from Khajjiar — close enough for an easy day trip, quiet enough to actually rest in the evening.
